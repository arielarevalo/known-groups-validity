# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN-... | Deprecated
- **Date proposed:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Date accepted:** YYYY-MM-DD (or `n/a` while Proposed)
- **Supersedes:** NNNN-... (if any)

## Context

The forcing function. Two or three short paragraphs: what is the situation,
what makes a decision necessary now, and what facts (results, code paths,
constraints, prices) the decision rests on. Cite specific files, line ranges,
results, or records by number. No editorialising.

## Decision

What we are doing. One or two paragraphs, imperative voice. Specific enough
that a reader can verify whether the code, configs, and plan are consistent
with the decision.

## Alternatives considered

Bullet list. Each alternative gets one sentence saying what it is and one
sentence saying why it lost. Include the "do nothing / status quo" option
when it was on the table.

## Consequences

What this decision enables, what it forbids, and what it risks. Use
sub-headings (`### Enables`, `### Forbids`, `### Risks`) when the lists are
long enough to need them; otherwise a single short list is fine. Be
specific: name the artifact, the code module, the cost line, or the design
DDR that is affected.

## References

- Files, code paths, or commits this decision touches.
- Prior operations records it depends on, and any `design/` DDR it is
  constrained by.
- External pricing pages or docs cited in Context.
